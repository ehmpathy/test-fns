# case=4 — EVERY catches a late flip (B5)

cells: 4 (jest + vitest, `[t3]`) · invariant: I3 · feel × care: sharp × criti

## .narrative

a developer runs locally with `criteria: 'EVERY'` to prove an LLM case is repeatable. attempt 1
passes, attempt 2 flips. the block fails. this holds **today** — `EVERY` registers plain tests with no
skip-on-success wrapper (`givenWhenThen.ts:392-394`), and the probe measured the block red. D3 is
therefore not a defect; B5 becomes a committed test that clamps it.
